Forensic Standards

How Every Investigation Is Built

1. Primary Archival Verification

No claim enters a case file without original parish registers, civil vital acts, population censuses, or municipal property books verified in state and diocesan archives.

2. Paleography & Borderland Context

18th- and 19th-century records from partitioned Poland span Polish, Latin, German, Russian, and Hebrew scripts—requiring careful decoding of phonetic surname shifts and changing imperial jurisdictions.

3. On-Location Field Documentation

Every investigation moves beyond paper files to the physical streets, tenement houses, parish churches, and cemeteries where ancestors actually lived.

About the Investigator

Author’s Note & Background

Janusz Stanisław Andrasz

Investigative Genealogist & Documentary Host
  • Creator of Genealogiczne Śledztwo (YouTube & Blog)
  • Genealogical Contributor, Halo tu Polsat (Polsat TV)
  • Former Private Secretary to the Radziwiłł Family (Portugal, 10 yrs)
  • Published Travel Author (ExpressMap)

I am an independent investigative genealogist, historical researcher, author, and documentary host based in Poland. My fascination with archival forensics began with my own family history—tracing the 17th-century Scottish merchant lineage of my ancestor Jakub (Jacobus) Watson across royal town books in Poznań, Bydgoszcz, Gdańsk, and Toruń, alongside my maternal roots in Volhynia and Galicia.

Before dedicating my work to historical and genealogical investigations, I spent ten years living in Portugal serving as private secretary to descendants of the historic Radziwiłł family of Nieśwież, and authored travel guidebooks on Portugal for ExpressMap. That decade immersed in European aristocratic archives and cross-cultural storytelling shaped the empirical approach I bring to every case today.

Through my podcast and research platform Genealogiczne Śledztwo (Genealogical Investigation) as well as television reportage segments created for Polsat, I uncover the forgotten Central and Eastern European chapters behind international artists, actors, and public figures—always grounding narrative storytelling in verifiable primary manuscripts.
